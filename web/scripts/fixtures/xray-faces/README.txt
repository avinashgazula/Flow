Photographs for the face recognition check (scripts/xray-faces-check.mjs) and the fixture prints in
src/features/player/xray/faces/prints.fixture.json.

Every one is a photograph taken by an employee of the U.S. federal government as part of their official duties, which
Wikimedia Commons lists as "Public domain" (17 U.S.C. section 105). Nothing here is under copyright, and no person is
shown in a way that asks for more than a portrait or group photograph of them at work. Licence lines are the ones
Wikimedia Commons shows on each file page, read through its API on 2026-11-11.

The portraits (obama, michelle, biden, clinton) stand in for TMDB profile photos, scaled to 632 pixels tall as TMDB's h632
size is. The two group photographs stand in for a frame of a film, at 1280 and 1920 pixels wide. They are used only to check
that the recogniser names actors in a picture; nobody's identity is being established, since the check is told who is who.
